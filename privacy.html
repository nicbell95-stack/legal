Privacy Policy

The Gentlemen's Playbook — last updated October 1, 2026

This policy explains what The Gentlemen's Playbook ("the app") collects, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. It is written against what the app actually does rather than from a template. The app is operated by Nicholas Bell ("we", "us").

Summary of key points

  • We store what you put into the app: your intake answers, the people you add, your notes about them, your Advisor conversations, and screenshots you upload for analysis.
  • We use one AI provider, Anthropic (Claude). Your content is sent to it to generate coaching, analysis and responses. It is not used to train AI models.
  • Photos you attach in Advisor and Concierge chats, outfit photos you send to What to Wear, and screenshots of your own profile you send to Profile Analysis are sent to Anthropic and are not stored on our servers.
  • Once each night the app sends your stored roster notes to Anthropic to refresh its summaries, whether or not you have the app open.
  • We send the venue searches you type (a city or zip code) to Google Places to find date spots. Your device location is never sent to Google.
  • For an outfit check in What to Wear we send approximate coordinates (about 1 km) and the date to Apple Weather for a forecast. With your permission the app also uses Apple's on-device geocoder to name your city. We do not store your location.
  • The Playbook video lessons stream from us. We do not record which lessons you watch.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • Deleting your account erases your uploaded screenshots immediately and removes your records from our database. See section 7.
  • The app is for adults 18 and over.

1. What we collect and why

Account information

When you create an account we store your email address and, if you sign up with a password, a hashed password. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, we receive the account identifier and email address that service returns. We also store a display name you choose.

Intake and profile

During onboarding we ask for and store: your date of birth, what you are looking for, your biggest challenge, dating experience, relationship status, current dating status, confidence level, where you meet women, your social disposition, and any free-text notes you add. We store your time zone and your subscription tier. Your date of birth is used to confirm you are 18 or over and is kept on your profile.

People you add

For each person on your roster we store the name you give them, your notes, an optional photo, a relationship stage and intent, and a computed confidence score along with its history. The app also builds and stores AI-generated notes, extracted facts, and a summary of that relationship. See section 5.

Your conversations and uploads

We store the full text of your Advisor conversations, your Roleplay sessions with their scores and improvement notes, your Concierge venue queries and the responses, screenshots you upload to Conversation Intel together with the resulting analysis, and any Clarity comparisons or reads you generate. We also store the daily briefs and challenges the app produces for you, and any feedback you submit.

We also store the text results of your Profile Analysis reviews (the scores, findings and suggested rewrites, including the prompt answers the review quotes) so a new review can be compared with your last one, and short text notes summarising your past outfit checks in What to Wear (what you were visibly wearing and the verdict), which are used to personalise future outfit advice. Neither contains your photos or screenshots.

Photos you attach

Photos you attach in Advisor and Concierge chats are sent to Anthropic to generate the reply and are not stored on our servers; the conversation we store records only that a photo was attached. The Advisor keeps its copies on your device so they appear when you return to the conversation. Concierge holds photos only for the conversation in progress and does not save them.

Outfit photos you send to What to Wear and screenshots of your own dating profile you send to Profile Analysis are sent to Anthropic for analysis and are not stored on our servers. If you continue a Profile Analysis review with the Advisor, those screenshots are kept on your device for that conversation.

Location and weather

When you use What to Wear with a date and a place, we send approximate coordinates (rounded to about 1 km) and the date to Apple Weather (WeatherKit) through our server, a Supabase edge function, and Apple returns a forecast. The place is the venue you picked, the city or zip code you typed, or, if you allow it, your device location. We do not store the coordinates or the forecast.

If you allow device location, the app uses Apple's on-device geocoder (an Apple Maps service) to turn your coordinates into a city name for display, which sends the coordinates to Apple. Device location is used only for the weather forecast and that city label. It is never sent to Google, and we keep no location history.

The Playbook lessons

The Playbook video lessons are our own content and stream to you from our servers. We do not record which lessons you open, how far through one you are, or when you watched it. No viewing history is stored.

Usage

We count how many times you use metered features (Advisor sessions, Intel uploads, Concierge lookups) so that plan limits can be enforced. We record whether you accepted the AI disclosure, and when.

What we do not collect

The app contains no analytics, advertising, attribution, tracking or crash-reporting software. We do not build advertising profiles and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. How your information is stored

Data is held in a hosted Supabase project — a Postgres database, authentication, and file storage.

Every table carries row-level security, so a signed-in account can read and write only its own rows. Screenshots you upload live in a private storage bucket, intel-screenshots, which is not publicly readable and is keyed by your user ID under the same rules. The Playbook video lessons are served from a separate public bucket; that bucket holds our lesson content only and contains nothing belonging to you.

Traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Calls to AI, venue and weather services are made from our server-side functions, so provider API keys are never shipped inside the app. The exception is Apple's geocoder, which turns your device location into a city name: it runs on your device, as part of iOS. The Apple Weather logo shown next to a forecast is also loaded directly from Apple.

4. AI processing

The Gentlemen's Playbook uses a third-party AI provider, Anthropic (Claude), to power its coaching features.

When you use Advisor, Concierge (including What to Wear), Conversation Intel, Profile Analysis, or Roleplay, the content you share (including your messages, photos you attach, uploaded screenshots, notes, and roleplay conversations) is sent to Anthropic to generate coaching, analysis, and responses.

Separately, the app refreshes its summaries on a nightly schedule. At approximately 3:00 AM and 4:00 AM UTC each day, the notes, extracted facts and existing summaries held against the people on your roster are sent to Anthropic to be rewritten. This happens automatically, whether or not you have the app open.

Your data is sent to Anthropic only to provide these features. It is not used to train AI models.

You are asked to agree to this before any content is sent. If you decline, the AI features do not run.

5. Information about other people

The app is built around recording information about people you know. When you add someone to your roster, write notes about them, upload a screenshot of a conversation with them, or generate an analysis, you are giving us personal information about a person who is not our user and who has not agreed to this policy.

We handle that information exactly as we handle the rest of your data: it is private to your account, covered by the same access rules, sent to Anthropic as described in section 4, and destroyed when you remove the person or delete your account. We do not contact these people, build independent profiles of them, or use their information for anything outside your account.

You are responsible for what you record about other people and for doing it lawfully. Do not upload someone else's private messages or images if you do not have the right to.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. Doing so runs a server-side deletion that, in order: removes every screenshot you uploaded from the private storage bucket, then deletes your authentication record, which cascades through the database and removes every row belonging to you — your profile and intake answers, everyone on your roster together with all notes, facts and summaries about them, your Advisor sessions and messages, Intel analyses, Profile Analysis results, outfit-check notes, Roleplay sessions, Concierge queries, daily briefs and challenges, score history, Clarity comparisons and reads, usage counters, and feedback. Photos the Advisor kept on your device are deleted from the device at the same time.

Your uploaded screenshots are removed from storage first, and our storage buckets are excluded from database backups, so once they are deleted no copy of them remains in our systems. Your database rows are removed from the live database immediately and are no longer reachable by the app or by us through it. Routine encrypted backups of the database may continue to hold copies for a short rolling period before those snapshots age out and are overwritten.

Two further things sit outside our systems entirely. Content already sent to Anthropic or Google is governed by that provider's own retention, and deleting your account here does not reach into their systems. Records that Apple or RevenueCat hold about a purchase are kept by them for their own financial and legal purposes.

8. Your rights

You can view and edit your profile and intake answers in the app, edit or delete any person on your roster and the notes attached to them, delete individual conversations and analyses, and delete your entire account and all its data. For anything you cannot do in the app — including a copy of your data in portable form — email us at the address in section 12 and we will respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, and to complain to a data protection authority. We do not charge for exercising these rights and we will not treat you differently for doing so.

Sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We have not done so in the preceding twelve months.

Do Not Track. The app contains no tracking or advertising software, so there is no cross-site tracking to signal about. We do not respond to Do Not Track browser signals.

Third parties. We do not permit third parties to collect personal information through the app for their own purposes.

Reviewing and changing your information. You may review and change your personal information at any time by signing in and opening Settings, or by emailing us.

10. Children

The Gentlemen's Playbook is for adults. You must be 18 or older to use it, and the app will not let you finish onboarding with a date of birth showing an age under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, email us and we will delete it.
