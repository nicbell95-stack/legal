Privacy Policy

The Lady's Choice — last updated September 24, 2026

This policy explains what The Lady's Choice ("the app") collects, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. It is written against what the app actually does rather than from a template. The app is operated by Nicholas Bell ("we", "us").

Summary of key points

  • We store what you put into the app: your intake answers, the people you add, your notes about them, your conversations, screenshots you upload, and your Vision boards.
  • Stylist does not keep your outfit photos. The photo is analysed and then discarded — we never write it to storage. What we keep is the written description of the outfit.
  • We use one AI provider, Anthropic (Claude). Your content is sent to it to produce the reads, analyses and replies. It is not used to train AI models.
  • Once each night the app sends your stored roster notes to Anthropic to refresh its summaries, whether or not you have the app open.
  • We send venue searches and, with your permission, your coordinates to Google Places to find date spots.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • Your data is stored in the United States and stays there.
  • Deleting your account erases your uploaded images immediately and your database records within seven days. See section 7 for exactly what that means.
  • The app is for adults 18 and over.

1. What we collect and why

Account information

When you create an account we store your email address and, if you sign up with a password, a hashed password. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, we receive the account identifier and email address that service returns. We also store a display name you choose.

Intake and profile

During onboarding we ask for and store: your date of birth, what you are looking for, your biggest challenge, dating experience, relationship status, current dating status, confidence level, where you meet people, your social disposition, and any free-text notes you add. We store your time zone and your subscription tier. Your date of birth is used to confirm you are 18 or over and is kept on your profile.

People you add

For each person you add we store the name you give them, your notes, an optional photo, a relationship stage and intent, and a computed confidence score along with its history. The app also builds and stores AI-generated notes, extracted facts, and a summary of that relationship. See section 5.

Your conversations and uploads

We store the full text of your Assistant conversations, your Roleplay sessions with their scores and improvement notes, your Concierge venue queries and the responses, screenshots you upload to Intel together with the resulting analysis, any comparisons or reads you generate, and the images and text you place on your Vision boards.

Stylist

When you use Stylist we store the occasion you describe and the surrounding detail you give about it, the conversation itself, and — for each outfit you show it — a written description of what you were wearing, the assessment of it, the garments and colours it identified, and its verdict.

We do not keep the photograph. The image is resized on your device, sent to our AI provider to be read, and never written to our file storage. A temporary copy stays in the app's cache on your own device while you are using the app, the same as any photo you open, and we never keep one. There is no stored photo to delete later, to leak, or to hand over. The written description is what takes its place, and it is what the app reads when it refers back to how you usually dress.

Those descriptions accumulate into a picture of your style, which is used to give more useful advice over time — noticing when something is a departure, or referring back to an outfit you wore before.

Usage

We count how many times you use metered features (Assistant sessions, Intel uploads, Concierge lookups, Stylist conversations and Stylist outfit reads) so that plan limits can be enforced. We record whether you accepted the AI disclosure, and when.

What we do not collect

The app contains no analytics, advertising, attribution, tracking or crash-reporting software. We do not build advertising profiles and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. How your information is stored

Data is held in a hosted Supabase project — a Postgres database, authentication, and file storage — located in the us-west-1 (West US, North California) region. Your data is stored in the United States and is not transferred outside it.

Every table carries row-level security, so a signed-in account can read and write only its own rows. Uploaded images live in two private storage buckets, intel-screenshots and vision-images, which are not publicly readable and are keyed by your user ID under the same rules. Stylist has no bucket at all, because it stores no photographs.

Traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Calls to AI and venue services are made from our server-side functions, so provider API keys are never shipped inside the app.

4. AI processing

The Lady's Choice uses a third-party AI provider, Anthropic (Claude). It reads what you share and writes what comes back.

When you use the Assistant, Concierge, Intel, Roleplay, or Stylist, the content you share — your messages, uploaded screenshots, outfit photos, notes, and roleplay conversations — is sent to Anthropic to produce the read, the analysis, or the reply.

An outfit photo is sent to Anthropic to be read and is not stored by us at any point. We cannot delete a copy held by our AI provider; their own retention governs that, as it does for everything else we send them.

Separately, the app refreshes its summaries on a nightly schedule. At approximately 3:00 AM and 4:00 AM UTC each day, the notes, extracted facts and existing summaries held against the people on your roster are sent to Anthropic to be rewritten. This happens automatically, whether or not you have the app open.

Your content goes to Anthropic only to make these features work. It is not used to train AI models.

You are asked to agree to this before any content is sent. If you decline, the AI features do not run.

5. Information about other people

The app is built around recording information about people you know. When you add someone, write notes about them, upload a screenshot of a conversation with them, or generate an analysis, you are giving us personal information about a person who is not our user and who has not agreed to this policy.

We handle that information exactly as we handle the rest of your data: it is private to your account, covered by the same access rules, sent to Anthropic as described in section 4, and destroyed when you remove the person or delete your account. We do not contact these people, build independent profiles of them, or use their information for anything outside your account.

You are responsible for what you record about other people and for doing it lawfully. Do not upload someone else's private messages or images if you do not have the right to.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. Doing so runs a server-side deletion that, in order: removes every image you uploaded from both private storage buckets, then deletes your authentication record, which cascades through the database and removes every row belonging to you — your profile and intake answers, everyone on your roster together with all notes, facts and summaries about them, your Assistant sessions and messages, Intel analyses, Roleplay sessions, Concierge queries, daily briefs and challenges, score history, comparisons and reads, Vision boards and layers, your Stylist conversations and every outfit description and assessment in them, usage counters, and feedback.

Two details matter, and we would rather state them plainly than imply the erasure is more absolute than it is:

  • Uploaded images are gone immediately. Our storage buckets are excluded from backups, so once the deletion removes your Intel screenshots and Vision board images there is no copy of them anywhere in our systems and they cannot be recovered, by you or by us.
  • Stylist's outfit photos were never there to delete. They are discarded the moment the analysis finishes and are never written to storage, so account deletion has nothing to remove. The written descriptions are database rows and follow the rule below.
  • Database records persist in backups for up to seven days. We take a daily backup of the database and keep each one for seven days. We do not use point-in-time recovery. Your rows are removed from the live database the moment you delete your account and are no longer reachable by the app or by us through it, but copies remain inside those rolling backup snapshots until the last snapshot taken before your deletion ages out — at most seven days, after which no copy remains.

Two further things sit outside our systems entirely. Content already sent to Anthropic or Google is governed by that provider's own retention and deleting your account here does not reach into their systems. Records that Apple or RevenueCat hold about a purchase are kept by them for their own financial and legal purposes.

8. Your rights

You can view and edit your profile and intake answers in the app, edit or delete any person on your roster and the notes attached to them, delete individual conversations, analyses and Vision boards, and delete your entire account and all its data. For anything you cannot do in the app — including a copy of your data in portable form — email us at the address in section 12 and we will respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, and to complain to a data protection authority. We do not charge for exercising these rights and we will not treat you differently for doing so.

9. California residents

Under the California Online Privacy Protection Act we disclose the following.

Categories collected. Identifiers (email address, account identifier, display name); personal information under Cal. Civ. Code §1798.80 (date of birth); commercial information (subscription tier and feature usage); geolocation data (only while you are using the venue finder, and only with your permission); audio, electronic and visual information (screenshots and images you upload, and outfit photos, which are processed and discarded rather than stored); and inferences drawn from your own content (confidence scores, relationship summaries, and descriptions of your style).

Sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We have not done so in the preceding twelve months.

Do Not Track. The app contains no tracking or advertising software, so there is no cross-site tracking to signal about. We do not respond to Do Not Track browser signals.

Third parties. We do not permit third parties to collect personal information through the app for their own purposes.

Reviewing and changing your information. You may review and change your personal information at any time by signing in and opening Settings, or by emailing us.

10. Children

The Lady's Choice is for adults. You must be 18 or older to use it, and the app will not let you finish onboarding with a date of birth showing an age under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, email us and we will delete it.
